Business Type: Beauty Salon

Service Promoted: Bridal Makeup

3. UGC-Style Video Script

Business: Priya Beauty Studio

Service: Bridal Makeup

Video Duration: 20–30 seconds

Style: Natural, friendly and attractive.

Scene 1 – Hook (0–5 seconds)

Visual: A bride getting ready for her wedding.

Voiceover: "Are you getting married soon? Looking for your perfect bridal look?"

Scene 2 – Problem (5–10 seconds)

Visual: A bride looking at different makeup styles.

Voiceover: "Choosing the right bridal makeup can be confusing!"

Scene 3 – Solution (10–20 seconds)

Visual: Makeup artist preparing bridal makeup and showing the finished look.

Voiceover: "At Priya Beauty Studio, we help you get a beautiful bridal look for your special day."

Scene 4 – Call to Action (20–30 seconds)

Visual: Bride smiling at the camera.

7. AI Prompts and Prompt Logic

Prompt 1: Generate UGC-Style Script

Prompt:

Act as a professional UGC content creator. Create a natural and engaging 30-second video script promoting bridal makeup services for Priya Beauty Studio. Include a hook, problem, solution and CTA. Use simple and friendly English.

Prompt Logic:
This prompt tells AI its role, business name, service, video duration and required content structure.

Prompt 2: Generate Multiple Hooks

Prompt:

Act as a social media marketing expert. Generate five attractive and scroll-stopping hooks for a bridal makeup service. Use short, emotional and engaging sentences.

Prompt Logic:
This prompt helps generate different opening lines to catch viewers' attention.

Prompt 3: Create Problem–Solution–CTA Content

Prompt:

Write promotional content for Priya Beauty Studio using the Problem–Solution–CTA format. Focus on brides-to-be and encourage them to book an appointment.

Prompt Logic:
This prompt identifies customer needs, presents the service as a solution and encourages booking.

Prompt 4: Adapt Content for Social Media

Prompt:

Adapt bridal makeup promotional content for Instagram Reels, Facebook posts and YouTube Shorts. Use suitable captions, titles and CTAs for each platform.

Prompt Logic:
This prompt helps AI adjust content according to each platform's format and audience.

Prompt 5: Match Brand Voice

Prompt:

Create marketing content for Priya Beauty Studio using a friendly, elegant and professional brand voice. Focus on customer interest and appointment bookings.

Prompt Logic:
This prompt maintains a consistent brand personality and creates persuasive marketing messages.
