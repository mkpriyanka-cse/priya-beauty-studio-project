7. Prompt Documentation

Prompt 1: Homepage Content Generation

Prompt Used:

Act as a professional website copywriter. Create attractive homepage content for Priya Beauty Studio, including a welcome headline, salon introduction, reasons to choose the salon, main services and a booking call-to-action.

Prompt Logic:

The prompt tells the AI the business name and business type. It gives clear instructions about the sections required on the homepage.

AI Output:

The AI generated a homepage with a welcome message, salon introduction, reasons to choose the salon, main services and an appointment booking message.

Prompt 2: Services Page Generation

Prompt Used:

Create a detailed Services Page for Priya Beauty Studio. Include haircut and hairstyling, facial and skincare, bridal makeup, manicure and pedicure, hair spa, waxing and threading. Explain each service and its benefits.

Prompt Logic:

The prompt provides a list of six services and asks the AI to explain their descriptions and customer benefits in simple English.

AI Output:

The AI generated six service descriptions with benefits for customers.

Prompt 3: CTA Content Generation

Prompt Used:

Create an attractive Call-to-Action section for Priya Beauty Studio. Include a catchy booking headline, friendly message, Book Appointment button, Contact Us message and closing sentence.

Prompt Logic:

The prompt instructs the AI to create persuasive messages that encourage website visitors to contact the salon and book appointments.

AI Output:

The AI generated a booking headline, appointment message, booking button text and contact message.

8. Adapting Content for Different Local Businesses

The same AI prompting method can be adapted for different types of local businesses by changing the business name, services and tone.

- Salon: Friendly, welcoming and beauty-focused language.
- Cafe: Warm, inviting and food-focused language.
- Clinic: Professional, clear and reassuring language.
- Agency: Creative, confident and service-focused language.

9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can help generate professional website content for local businesses. Using ChatGPT, we created homepage content, service descriptions and call-to-action messages for Priya Beauty Studio.

The project helped us understand prompt engineering, website copywriting, customer-focused communication and AI content generation.
